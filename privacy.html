Privacy Policy

SignalScout · Effective November 21, 2026

SignalScout ("the app") is made by Justin Hemphill. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it. The short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your location is used only to look up coverage.

Location you choose to check

  • Typed addresses are turned into map coordinates by Apple's built-in geocoding service on your iPhone, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • "Use my current location" asks for iOS location permission. Your location is read once, only when you tap the button. The app doesn't track your location in the background.
  • The coordinates for the spot you're checking are sent to our coverage lookup service, hosted by Supabase, to find carrier coverage around that point. We don't store your searches or link them to you. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs, which can include those coordinates, for a short time for security and troubleshooting.

What we don't collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number is required.
  • No advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information.

Coverage data

Coverage results come from public data carriers report to the Federal Communications Commission. This product uses the FCC Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the FCC.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
